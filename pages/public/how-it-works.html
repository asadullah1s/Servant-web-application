How It Works

For Employers

  1. 1. Register - Create your employer account.
  2. 2. Search - Find workers by category, city, and skills.
  3. 3. Review Profiles - Check verified badges and experience.
  4. 4. Send Request - Submit a hiring request.
  5. 5. Connect - Get in touch and finalize the deal.

For Workers

  1. 1. Register - Fill your biodata in Urdu/English.
  2. 2. Upload Documents - Submit CNIC and certificates.
  3. 3. Get Verified - Admin verifies your profile.
  4. 4. Apply - Apply for jobs matching your skills.
  5. 5. Start Working - Get hired and start earning.
