Safety & Verification

We ensure that every worker on our platform is trusted and verified.

1. Identity Verification

Every worker submits a valid CNIC/ID which is verified by our admin team.

2. Contact Verification

Phone numbers and WhatsApp are verified through OTP before approval.

3. Document Review

Experience certificates and previous work records are reviewed manually.

4. Verified Badge

Only verified profiles get the ✓ Verified badge.

5. Ratings & Reviews

Employers can rate workers, ensuring transparency and trust.

6. Data Privacy

Sensitive information like CNIC numbers are masked and protected.
